About · News · Research · Publications · Awards · Experience

I'm a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Texas at El Paso, supervised by Dr. Md Maruf Hossain Shuvo. My research focuses on building efficient, deployable AI systems through model compression and algorithm–hardware co-design.

I work on energy-efficient pruning for long-context large language models, end-to-end transformer inference on edge FPGAs, and using LLMs to improve RTL quality and FPGA power-performance-area. More broadly, I'm interested in efficient AI, AI accelerator design, and LLMs for chip design.

Before UTEP, I received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET (major: Communication and Signal Processing) and worked as a Radio Service Specialist Engineer at Banglalink.
